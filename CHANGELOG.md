# Changelog

## 1.6.2 — 2026-11-11 (versionCode 12)

• The Calendar now marks every day you wrote a journal entry with a small badge, so you can find them at a glance (break and build habits)
• Build habits show that day's journal entries on the Calendar too
• Refreshed store screenshots and description


## 1.6.1 — 2026-11-11 (versionCode 11)

• Add any event to your home screen straight from the app: long-press an event card, use the editor menu, or tap "Add to home screen" when you create one
• "Add a widget" on the Events screen shows your next event automatically
• Freshly placed Event widgets now show up right away


## 1.6.0 — 2026-11-11 (versionCode 10)

• Events: count down to dates, count time since, or run timers, with built-in Today / Week / Month / Year progress
• 5 card designs, 10 fonts, custom colours and icons, with a live preview as you edit
• New home-screen Event widget: pick an event or auto-show the next one, with full, translucent or transparent backgrounds
• Optional event notifications, calendar import (only when you choose it) and a "Give me ideas" starter list
• Fixed the Ring widget not updating after the day changes
• All your habits, slips and history are untouched


## 1.5.1 — 2026-11-11 (versionCode 9)

• Sharper widgets: the ring and heatmap are now drawn at your screen's full resolution instead of being stretched
• The heatmap widget keeps its squares square when resized


## 1.5.0 — 2026-11-11 (versionCode 8)

• New "Not a slip" option when logging: save an involuntary or not-a-slip moment as a private note that never touches your streak
• Notes show on the Calendar with a small dot on that day
• Included in backups; older backups still restore
• All existing habits, slips and history are untouched


## 1.4.0 — 2026-11-10 (versionCode 7)

• Build habits, not just break them: drink water, read, work out, meditate, walk and more
• Daily goals (e.g. 3 L of water) or simple check-offs, on every day or a custom weekly schedule
• Reminders every 30–180 minutes within your active hours, or at set times; log, snooze or skip right from the notification
• Streaks, completion rate, missed days and weekly history for build habits
• All existing habits, slips and history are untouched


## 1.3.2 — 2026-11-10 (versionCode 6)

• Friendlier starting suggestions: Alcohol, Smoking, Doomscrolling, Sugar, Gaming and more
• Removed sensitive suggestions and triggers from the setup screens


## 1.3.1 — 2026-11-08 (versionCode 5)

• Widgets always show their own layout: a progress ring no longer turns into the slim streak counter after unlocking or after a while.
• The live h:m:s clock on widgets is never cut off.


## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-07 (versionCode 4)

• Add up to 3 photos to a journal entry. They stay private inside Daymark, never in your gallery, and are included in backups (you can turn that off)
• Widgets now fill their whole space, with sharper sizing for every widget
• Heatmap and progress ring scale to the widget's real size

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-07 (versionCode 3)

• Ride out an urge: log cravings you got through, and what helped
• Journal on any day, and keep your own reasons close
• Money kept, and a cut-down mode with a daily limit
• Daily check-in and milestone reminders
• New widgets: Quick actions and Your progress; refreshed widget design
• Discreet mode: nothing on your home screen names what you track
• Password-protected and automatic backups
• Quick Settings tile, app shortcuts, share a milestone

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-04 (versionCode 2)

- Multiple trackers, each with its own streaks, calendar, insights and achievements (Room v2 migration)
- Home-screen widgets: Streak (live counter), Progress ring, Heatmap, Daily motivation, in 6 styles, with a per-widget tracker
- Eight accent colours (Dusk indigo is the default) and an indigo launcher icon
- Fixed unreadable text in dark mode on the onboarding and lock screens
- Backup format v2 (reads v1)
- Targets Android 16 (API 36), plus Play Store listing assets

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-04 (versionCode 1)

- First release: dashboard, calendar, slip logging, insights, widget, app lock, backup and restore, themes
