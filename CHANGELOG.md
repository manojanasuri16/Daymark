# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-07 (versionCode 3)

• Ride out an urge: log cravings you got through, and what helped
• Journal on any day, and keep your own reasons close
• Money kept, and a cut-down mode with a daily limit
• Daily check-in and milestone reminders
• New widgets: Quick actions and Your progress; refreshed widget design
• Discreet mode: nothing on your home screen names what you track
• Password-protected and automatic backups
• Quick Settings tile, app shortcuts, share a milestone

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-04 (versionCode 2)

- Multiple trackers, each with its own streaks, calendar, insights and achievements (Room v2 migration)
- Home-screen widgets: Streak (live counter), Progress ring, Heatmap, Daily motivation, in 6 styles, with a per-widget tracker
- Eight accent colours (Dusk indigo is the default) and an indigo launcher icon
- Fixed unreadable text in dark mode on the onboarding and lock screens
- Backup format v2 (reads v1)
- Targets Android 16 (API 36), plus Play Store listing assets

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-04 (versionCode 1)

- First release: dashboard, calendar, slip logging, insights, widget, app lock, backup and restore, themes
