Daymark is a calm, private streak tracker for anyone working on changing a habit. Every day you mark is a day you made, and a slip never erases what you've built.

Your data stays on your phone. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission.

TRACK MORE THAN ONE THING
• Separate trackers for each habit you're changing, such as alcohol, smoking or doomscrolling
• Every tracker keeps its own streaks, calendar, insights and achievements
• Switch between trackers with one tap

RIDE OUT URGES
• "Feeling an urge?" — log a craving you got through, what set it off and what helped
• Your own reasons, shown right when you need them
• A journal for any day, with a gentle prompt
• Money kept: see what you no longer spend
• Cut-down mode: set a daily limit instead of quitting outright
• Optional daily check-in and milestone reminders
• Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts

TODAY
• A live streak counter showing days, plus ticking hours, minutes and seconds
• A progress ring toward your next milestone
• Your longest streak and total clean days at a glance
• A gentle daily encouragement

HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
• Streak: a live ticking counter
• Progress Ring: see your next milestone without opening the app
• Heatmap: the last weeks of clean and slip days
• Daily Motivation: your own reasons or a short, supportive message
• Quick Actions: ride out an urge, journal or log in one tap
• Your Progress: money kept, urges ridden out and more
• Six styles for every widget: Dusk, Dawn, Forest, Midnight, Sunrise and Minimal
• Each widget can show the tracker you choose

ACHIEVEMENTS
• 12 milestones, from 24 hours to 5 years
• Earned against your longest streak, so they are never taken away after a slip

CALENDAR
• A swipeable month view: clean days in green, slips in red, untracked days in grey
• Tap any day to see details, or to log or edit a slip

LOG A SLIP, LEARN FROM IT
• Record the date, time, mood, triggers, situation and notes
• Edit or delete entries, and undo a delete
• Slips are information, not judgment

INSIGHTS
• Streak history and slips per month
• Common triggers, time-of-day and weekday patterns
• Mood before a slip and the situations involved
• A supportive trend summary

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics
• No internet permission: the app cannot send your data anywhere
• Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or device PIN, handled by Android
• Optional "hide in recents" blanks the app preview and blocks screenshots
• Discreet mode: widgets and notifications never name what you're tracking
• Android cloud backup is turned off
• Backup and restore using a file you control, optionally password-protected
• Optional automatic daily backups to a folder you choose

YOUR LOOK
• Light, dark or system theme
• Eight accent colors, or Material You colors from your wallpaper
• Smooth, gentle motion that respects your system animation settings

Daymark is a self-tracking tool. It is not a medical service and is not a substitute for professional help. If you are struggling, please consider reaching out to a doctor, counselor or support service in your area.
