Daymark is a calm, private habit app for building good habits, breaking unwanted ones and counting down to what matters. Every day you mark is a day you made, and a slip or a missed day never erases what you have built.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission.

## What's new in 1.6
- **Events:** countdowns, time since and timers, with built-in Today, This week, This month and This year progress cards
- **Not a slip:** save an involuntary or not-a-slip moment as a private note that never touches your streak
- **Sharper widgets:** the ring and heatmap are drawn at your screen's full resolution
- **Add to home screen from inside the app:** long-press an event card or use the editor menu
- **Journal days on the Calendar:** a badge marks every day you journalled

## Build a habit
- Presets such as drink water, read, work out, meditate, walk, stretch, journal or sleep earlier, or make your own
- Daily goals with a unit (3 L of water, 10 pages) or a simple "mark done"
- Every day or a weekly schedule; rest days never break a streak
- Reminders every 30 to 180 minutes within the hours you choose, or at fixed times. Log, snooze or skip right from the notification
- Streaks over your scheduled days, completion rate, missed days, weekly history, a calendar and insights
- Fix a forgotten day from the calendar

## Break a habit
- A live streak that ticks in days, hours, minutes and seconds, with a progress ring toward your next milestone
- Longest streak and total clean days at a glance
- A slip never erases history: your longest streak, clean days and achievements are always kept
- Quit completely, or cut down with a daily limit. Only the entry that takes a day over your limit counts as a slip
- Money kept, your own reasons, and urges you rode out
- Log a slip with date, time, mood, triggers, situation and a note. Edit or delete entries, and undo a delete
- Choose "Not a slip" to keep a note without touching your streak
- 12 milestones from 24 hours to 5 years, earned against your longest streak. Share a milestone as an image drawn on your phone
- Insights: streak history, slips per month, triggers, time-of-day and weekday patterns, mood before a slip

## Journal, Calendar and Insights
- A journal for any day, with a mood, a gentle prompt and up to three photos that stay inside Daymark
- A swipeable calendar: clean days in green, slips in red, untracked days in grey, with a dot on days that have a note and a small badge on every day you wrote a journal entry, so you can always find them (build habits get the same badge and show that day's entries)
- Track as many habits as you like. Each tracker keeps its own streaks, calendar, insights and achievements

## Events
- Count down to a birthday, trip or deadline, count the time since a fresh start, or run a timer
- Built-in Today, This week, This month and This year progress
- Repeat daily, weekly, monthly or yearly
- Five card designs (Text, Ring, Grid, Bars, Minimal), ten fonts, custom colours and icons, with a live preview as you edit
- Notes, around 60 starter ideas and a short first-run wizard
- Optional notifications when an event arrives, a day or week before, and milestones after
- Share any card as an image
- Optional one-time import from your calendar, only when you ask

## Widgets
- Streak, Progress ring, Heatmap, Daily motivation, Quick actions and Your progress
- An Event widget with three backgrounds (full, translucent, transparent), or an upcoming queue that shows your next event
- Add any event to your home screen from inside the app
- Six styles: Dusk, Dawn, Forest, Midnight, Sunrise and Minimal. Each widget can show the tracker you choose

## Private by design
- Works fully offline. Your data never leaves the device
- Discreet mode (on by default): widgets, notifications and share cards never name what you track
- Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or device PIN, handled by Android
- Optional "hide in recents" blanks the app preview and blocks screenshots
- Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts for urge, journal and log
- The only extra permissions are notifications for the reminders you switch on, and an optional calendar read that happens only when you tap "Import from calendar"

## Your look and your data
- Light, dark or system theme, eight accent colours, or Material You colours from your wallpaper
- Backup and restore with a JSON file you control, optionally protected with a password (AES-256-GCM)
- Optional automatic daily backups to a folder you choose, keeping the last seven
- Android cloud backup is turned off
